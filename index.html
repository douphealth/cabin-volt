2026 Aviation Power Bank Rule — Quick Answer

Under the ICAO March 27, 2026 amendment, passengers may carry a maximum of 2 power banks in cabin baggage only (never in checked luggage), rated ≤ 100 Wh without approval or 100–160 Wh with airline approval. Recharging power banks during flight is prohibited, recalled models are barred regardless of Wh, and carriers such as AEGEAN (A3) and Emirates (EK) enforce a stricter 1-bank limit.
